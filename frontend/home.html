ABOUT THE PROJECT

Turning public complaints into actionable insights.

Smart Governance combines natural language processing, machine learning and explainable AI to support civic complaint analysis. It helps organise complaints, estimate severity and present recommendations while keeping the final decision in the hands of the responsible officer.
